How it works

From crypto to cash in four steps

OffRamp is a manual off-ramp: every payout is reviewed and processed by our team, so your funds go exactly where they should.

1

Enter the amount

Pick the asset you want to sell — Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, USDT or USDC — and type the amount. You'll see exactly how many US dollars you'll receive at the live market rate, with no fees added.

2

Choose how to get paid

Send your dollars to a debit card for a near-instant payout, or to a US checking or savings account by bank transfer. You choose whichever works best for you.

3

Confirm your details

Enter your payout details and billing information. Your rate is locked the moment you confirm, so market swings between confirmation and payout won't change what you receive.

4

Get paid

Send your crypto to the deposit address we provide. Once it's received and reviewed, we release your dollars — within minutes for debit cards, or 1–3 business days for bank transfers.

Good to know

Zero conversion fees

We don't add a percentage on top of your conversion. The live rate you see is the rate you're paid, minus only the network fee your blockchain charges to send the crypto.

Rates locked at confirmation

Crypto prices move constantly. When you confirm an order, we lock the quoted rate so the dollar amount you agreed to is the amount you receive.

Reviewed by real people

Every payout is manually verified before funds are released. It's an extra step that keeps both sides protected against fraud and mistaken transfers.
