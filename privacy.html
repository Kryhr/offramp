Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OffRamp ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, discloses and protects your information when you use our website and crypto-to-fiat conversion service (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

To process a payout, we collect the information you provide directly, which may include:

  • Identity and contact details — your name, email address and billing address.
  • Payout details — for debit card payouts: your card number, expiry date and security code. For bank transfers: your bank name, account type, routing number and account number.
  • Transaction details — the asset and amount you sell, the wallet address funds are sent from, the rate applied and your order reference.
  • Technical data — basic device and connection information, such as IP address and browser type, collected automatically when you visit the site.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Process and settle your payout to your chosen card or bank account.
  • Verify transactions and protect against fraud, error and unauthorized activity.
  • Communicate with you about your orders and respond to support requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal, tax and anti-money-laundering obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How we store and protect your data

Information is transmitted over an encrypted (TLS) connection. Sensitive payout details are used only to complete your transaction and are handled under access controls that limit who can view them. We retain personal and financial information only as long as necessary to provide the Service, resolve disputes and meet our legal obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

4. When we share information

We share information only as needed to run the Service, including with payment processors and banking partners who execute your payout, and with authorities where required by law or valid legal process. We require any service provider handling your data to protect it and use it only for the purpose we specify.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact us at the address below. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

6. Cookies

We use only the cookies and local storage necessary for the site to function. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking for marketing.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you.

8. Contact
